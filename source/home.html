My Background

A Stanford Alum and former Silicon Valley Tech Startup COO, I come to the table with over 20 years of experience serving and assisting others with achieving their goals and improving their overall well-being. 


My approach is holistic and uniquely tailored to each individual's particular circumstances, interests and needs.

My Philosophy

My Background

My Background

I believe that everyone has the potential to live a joyous,  fulfilled and meaningful life - especially with the appropriate level of support, confidence & tools. 


By identifying and working towards goals intentionally and collaboratively, my clients are able to unlock their full potential more quickly, creating - and savoring -  a life they love.
